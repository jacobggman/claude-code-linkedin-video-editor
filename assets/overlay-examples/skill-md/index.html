6# Video Edit
7
8Drop your raw clips in a folder and run
/video-edit.
9
10## Workflow
111. Transcribe every clip with word-level
timestamps
122. Find the cleanest take of each line, cut
false starts and filler
133. Assemble the cut at 1080x1920, punch-ins
from 4K
144. Cut the creator out of the background (for
text behind them)
155. Apply the aesthetic, captions and motion
graphics
166. Add sound design, render, check every cut
frame by frame
17
18## Aesthetics
19- **cool girl**: warm faded grade, lowercase
sans + big serif italic in butter yellow
#FAE67A, handwritten notes, sparkles
20- **cool dude**: dark moody grade, tiny
wide-tracked caps + heavy serif punch words
21- **bold**: heavy lowercase words scattered
around the speaker, big words behind the head
22
23## Tools
24- whisper for word-level transcripts
25- ffmpeg for cuts, grades and the final render
26- hyperframes for captions and motion graphics
27
28## Rules
29- Never cover the face
30- Captions inside Instagram safe zones
31- Words land on the exact spoken frame
32- Sound effects subtle, never louder than the
voice
33
34## Output
35- reel.mp4, 1080x1920, 30fps
36- a phone-size preview for quick review
37- an edit log with every cut and why
